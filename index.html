About Us

Company Introduction

Samriddhi Agro Trading and Exim LLP is an agri-commodity trading and exim company working across the agricultural value chain. We procure from farmers, FPOs, traders and processors, check quality against the buyer's specification, and deliver by truck, railway rake or container. Our focus is simple: the right grade, on time, with transparent paperwork — as a trader our customers can rely on, deal after deal.

FAQ

Common questions

Which commodities do you supply?

Cereals & grains, oilseeds, pulses, cotton, spices & dry fruits, and flour & processed products. Availability, specifications, quantities and terms are confirmed per enquiry.

How is the material packed?

Loose (bulk/rake) or in 50 kg PP/jute bags, as agreed with the buyer.

How do you deliver?

By truck, railway rake or container, with transparent paperwork.

What documents come with a delivery?

GST invoice, e-way bill and quality report with each delivery. Export documentation is handled for overseas orders.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Specifications and MOQ are shared on request — tell us your commodity, grade, quantity, location and date.

I'm a farmer, FPO or trader. Can I supply to you?

Yes. We offer market-linked prices, clear quality parameters, timely agreed payment and long-term repeat procurement. Message us on WhatsApp to start.
