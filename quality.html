Our standards

Quality & Compliance

Quality is agreed before the trade, checked before dispatch, and documented with every delivery.

  • Specifications agreed in writing for every order (moisture, foreign matter, damaged grain, protein/oil content as relevant)
  • Sampling and lab testing at NABL-accredited or buyer-approved labs where required
  • Pre-dispatch inspection and loading supervision
  • Weighment at approved weighbridges
  • Lot-wise traceability to source where available
  • GST-compliant invoicing and e-way bills; export documentation as applicable
