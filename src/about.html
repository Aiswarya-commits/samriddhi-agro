Who we are

Company Profile

Samriddhi Agro Trading and Exim LLP is an agricultural commodity trading and export enterprise. We procure cereals, oilseeds, pulses, cotton, spices and processed agro products from farmers, FPOs, traders and processors, and supply them to processors, millers and institutional buyers in India and abroad.

We work as a trader — taking responsibility for sourcing, quality, logistics and documentation — so our buyers get dependable supply and our suppliers get fair, timely payment.

Our mission

Mission

To build an efficient link between agricultural producers and markets by delivering dependable sourcing, quality-focused trade execution, efficient logistics and responsive solutions for buyers and suppliers.

Our vision

Vision

To become a trusted and efficient agricultural trading partner, connecting Indian commodities with domestic and global markets through quality, innovation, responsible sourcing and long-term partnerships.
